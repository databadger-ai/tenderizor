SYNTHETIC DEVELOPMENT FIXTURE — NOT AN OFFICIAL CANADABUYS RECORD

Solicitation: DEMO-2026-001
Title: Supply and delivery of rugged laptops
Buyer: Example Federal Department
Publication date: 2026-08-01
Question deadline: 2026-08-14 14:00 America/Toronto
Closing deadline: 2026-08-28 14:00 America/Toronto
Contract term: One year with two optional one-year extensions.

1. Scope
The contractor must supply 120 rugged laptops and deliver them to Ottawa, Ontario within 60 calendar days after contract award.

2. Mandatory requirements
M1. The bidder must be registered to conduct business in Canada at bid closing.
M2. The proposed laptop must include at least 32 GB RAM, a 1 TB solid-state drive, Wi-Fi 6E, and a three-year manufacturer warranty.
M3. The bidder must provide evidence of two completed contracts in the last five years for delivery of at least 50 comparable devices.
M4. A bid security of CAD 25,000 must accompany the bid.

3. Rated requirements
R1. Up to 20 points are available for delivery earlier than 60 calendar days.
R2. Up to 15 points are available for an extended warranty beyond three years.

4. Basis of selection
The responsive bid with the highest combined technical and price score will be recommended for award. Mandatory requirements are evaluated before rated requirements.

5. Submission
Technical and financial proposals must be uploaded as separate PDF files through the authorized tendering portal before the closing deadline. Late submissions will not be accepted.

6. Clarification
The notice does not publish whether equivalent manufacturer certifications will be accepted. Questions must be submitted before the question deadline.
